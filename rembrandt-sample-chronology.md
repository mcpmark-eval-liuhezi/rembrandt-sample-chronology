## Observations for the talk
The sample spans Rembrandt's career from age ~20 (1626) to his late period (1665). The Night Watch (1642) sits in the middle, with a notable 18-year gap between it (1642) and the next dated works in this sample (1660) — the sample is deliberately capped at 10 works, so this gap partly reflects sampling rather than a true stylistic hiatus. The sample is dense in the 1630s and 1660s, with just one work each in the 1620s and 1640s, and none in the 1650s.
